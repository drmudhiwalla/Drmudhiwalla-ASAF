Health Screening Disclaimer

Last Updated: 17 September 2026

At DrMudhiwalla Healthtech Private Limited, our health screening services are designed to support preventive health awareness, early identification of potential health risks and informed health decisions. Please read the following before participating in a screening or relying on a screening result.

1. Screening is not diagnosis

Read More Close

Our screening services are not a medical diagnosis and do not replace consultation, examination or treatment by a qualified healthcare professional.

Depending on the programme, screening may include measurements such as blood pressure, blood glucose, cholesterol, BMI, BRI, waist measurements, lifestyle, sleep, stress, physical activity and other health-related assessments.

2. Screening results are indicative

Read More Close

A screening result, score or Green / Yellow / Red risk category reflects an assessment based on the information and measurements available at the time of screening.

It does not confirm the presence or absence of a disease or medical condition.

A result indicating lower risk does not guarantee that you are free from a health condition. Similarly, a higher-risk result does not by itself establish a diagnosis.

3. Results may have limitations

Read More Close

Screening results can be influenced by:

  • The circumstances at the time of measurement;
  • Device or measurement variability;
  • Temporary physiological factors;
  • Information provided by the participant;
  • Incomplete or inaccurate information; and
  • Limitations of the screening methodology.

Where appropriate, further clinical evaluation or diagnostic testing may be recommended.

4. Do not change treatment based solely on screening

Read More Close

You should not start, stop or change any medication, treatment or other medical intervention solely on the basis of our screening results or risk assessment.

Please consult a qualified healthcare professional for personalised medical advice.

5. If you have symptoms

Read More Close

Our screening service is not an emergency medical service.

If you are experiencing symptoms, feel unwell, or believe you require urgent medical attention, seek appropriate medical or emergency care immediately rather than relying on a screening result.

6. Your health information

Read More Close

Information collected during a screening may include personal and health-related information.

How we collect, use, share, retain and protect such information is explained in our Privacy Policy and the applicable consent notice for the screening programme.

7. Partner locations

Read More Close

Screenings may be conducted at banks, gyms, workplaces, community locations or other partner establishments.

The fact that a screening is conducted at a partner location does not mean that the partner automatically receives your detailed health information. Any sharing of information will be governed by the applicable programme, consent, Privacy Policy and applicable law.

IMPORTANT
A HEALTH SCREENING IS A PREVENTIVE AWARENESS TOOL — NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR MEDICAL DIAGNOSIS OR PROFESSIONAL MEDICAL CARE.

By participating in a DrMudhiwalla health screening, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this disclaimer and that screening results should be interpreted in their appropriate context.
