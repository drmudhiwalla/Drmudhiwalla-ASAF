IPAQ Classification Criteria / वर्गीकरण मानदंड

  • Low (Inactive) — Does not meet criteria for categories 2 or 3
  • Moderately Active — 3+ days vigorous (20+ min/day) OR 5+ days moderate/walking (30+ min/day) OR 5+ days any combination achieving 600+ MET-min/week
  • Highly Active — 3+ days vigorous (20+ min/day, totalling 1500+ MET-min/week) OR 7+ days any activity (3000+ MET-min/week)
Based on the International Physical Activity Questionnaire (IPAQ) Short Form.
