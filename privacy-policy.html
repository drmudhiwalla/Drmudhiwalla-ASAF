This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through our website, digital platforms, health screening programmes, assessments and related services, unless a separate privacy notice is provided for a particular service.

Our Commitment to Your Health Data

Your health data is entrusted to us. We treat it as a responsibility, not a commodity. Health information is different from ordinary contact information. A blood-pressure reading, blood-sugar result, lifestyle response or health-risk assessment can reveal something meaningful about an individual.

We therefore seek to follow these principles:

Collect only what is necessary. We seek to collect information reasonably required to provide the relevant service.
Use it for clearly communicated purposes. We will explain why information is being collected and use it consistently with the purposes communicated to you, subject to applicable law.
Share only what is necessary. Participation in a screening programme at a bank, gym, workplace or other partner location does not automatically give that organisation unrestricted access to your detailed health information.
Protect what is entrusted to us. We implement reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information we process.
Do not sell identifiable health information. We do not sell identifiable personal health information as a commercial product.
Use aggregated information responsibly. Where permitted by applicable law, appropriately aggregated or anonymised information may be used to understand population-level trends, evaluate programmes, improve services and support research.

What Health Information Do We Collect?

Read More Close

Depending on the screening or service, health-related information may include:

Physical measurements

  • Height; Weight; Body Mass Index (BMI); Body Roundness Index (BRI); Waist circumference; Waist-to-height or other relevant measurements.

Vital measurements

Assessment outputs

We may also generate: Screening results; Health scores; Lifestyle scores; Risk categories; Preventive-health recommendations; and Reports generated from the information collected during the assessment.
