Your Data Stays Yours

Consent-first and confidential. Only your final Green/Yellow/Red category may be shared with gym management — your detailed health information remains private.

Your WhatsApp number is your identifier for your report. It is never shared with any third party.

This screening is for health awareness only and does not replace medical diagnosis or consultation.
